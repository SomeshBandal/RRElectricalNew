# Bright architectural website redesign

## What will change
- Replace the dark sitewide background with the selected Pearl and Citrus system: pearl white, pale sage, deep green, and restrained gold accents.
- Apply the chosen Architectural High-Key Minimalism direction across the homepage, services, products, work, contact, navigation, footer, and loading screen.
- Preserve all existing business copy, media, product categories, filtering, routes, SEO, and automatic bulb-loader behavior.

## Page and card design
- Keep the homepage video immersive, but brighten its treatment and integrate it into a light architectural frame with the two existing centered actions.
- Restructure repeated content into a responsive bento rhythm: larger priority modules, compact supporting tiles, and intentional variation without nested cards.
- Restyle product and project cards with crisp pearl surfaces, fine sage borders, short gold edge traces, sharper image crops, and subtle dimensional shadows.
- Refresh filters, forms, buttons, page banners, statistics, and calls to action so every page shares one coherent visual language.

## Motion and finish
- Use quick engineered motion: border traces, current sweeps, masked image reveals, gentle elevation, and tactile action states.
- Keep animation restrained, seamless, mobile-safe, and disabled when reduced motion is preferred.
- Verify all routes at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, filters, product expansion, form controls, loader, media loading, overflow, and browser errors.

## Technical details
- Replace the global semantic color, shadow, radius, typography, and surface tokens with the exact selected palette and Space Grotesk / DM Sans pairing.
- Update shared elements first, then page-specific layouts so visual behavior stays consistent.
- Keep all imagery local and deployment-safe, preserve current metadata, and retain responsive image loading optimizations.
