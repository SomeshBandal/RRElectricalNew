# ServiceTitan-inspired full-site redesign

## What will change
- Rebuild the shared site shell to closely follow the reference’s hierarchy: slim utility strip, spacious white navigation, prominent quote action, large rounded feature panels, trust signals, and a structured footer.
- Redesign Home, Services, Products, Work, and Contact as a cohesive family while retaining all R R Enterprises branding, copy, product catalogues, project imagery, contact details, URLs, SEO, and the automatic bulb loader.
- Match the reference’s commercial polish, proportions, spacing, bold typography, high-contrast media treatment, rounded panels, strong calls to action, and restrained motion without copying its trademarked assets, wording, customer logos, or software graphics.

## Page treatment
- **Home:** large photographic electrical-project hero with headline, two actions, credibility facts, service highlights, product ranges, selected work, statistics, and a strong survey call to action.
- **Services:** bold introductory panel, sector-led service modules, delivery process, and tender enquiry action.
- **Products:** reference-style category navigation and structured product cards, preserving all Legrand and Vinay ranges and expandable lists.
- **Work:** large project-led imagery, sector filters, project cards, and conversion panel.
- **Contact:** clear contact details beside a refined enquiry form with a prominent reassurance section.

## Visual system
- Keep Space Grotesk headings and DM Sans body text.
- Move to a clean warm-white page shell with deep charcoal feature areas, controlled copper/coral calls to action, crisp gray borders, and subtle electrical-blue details.
- Use large-radius media panels, smaller-radius functional cards, quiet shadows, image overlays, and selective electrical shimmer instead of pervasive glowing effects.
- Preserve accessible focus states, mobile navigation, reduced-motion behavior, and strong contrast.

## Technical details
- Refactor shared header, footer, page banner, buttons, cards, filters, and section wrappers first so all routes inherit the new system.
- Recompose each route using existing local images and data; optimize image sizing, lazy loading, and stable layout dimensions.
- Keep every existing route’s unique metadata and verify the loader, mobile menu, product expansion, work filters, contact form, and navigation.
- Validate desktop and mobile layouts, browser console, image loading, keyboard focus, and final preview build.
