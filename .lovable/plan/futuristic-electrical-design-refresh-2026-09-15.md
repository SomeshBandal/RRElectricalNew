# Futuristic electrical design refresh

## What will change
- Refresh the entire site with a premium “energized editorial” direction: deep graphite foundations, luminous electric cyan, warm filament amber, and restrained mint/blush accents.
- Make the navigation, page banners, section transitions, cards, filters, forms, footer, and loading experience feel like one cohesive system.
- Preserve all existing copy, product organization, project imagery, contact details, routes, and the bulb-switch opening interaction.
- Add refined electrical motion: subtle circuit traces, scanning highlights, animated energy glows, image treatment, and responsive hover/focus states.
- Keep every page readable and polished on mobile and desktop, including reduced-motion support.

## Visual direction
- Use dark technical surfaces for high-impact areas, bright neutral surfaces for content-heavy sections, and pastel colors only as controlled accent signals.
- Replace the current soft pastel-card repetition with sharper glass-and-metal surfaces, luminous edge treatments, compact labels, and stronger hierarchy.
- Keep amber tied to the bulb/filament identity while using cyan for active controls, technical details, and energy effects.

## Technical details
- Expand the semantic color, surface, shadow, gradient, and animation tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Update shared header, page banner, footer, divider, and logo presentation first so every route inherits the refreshed identity.
- Restyle the home, services, products, work, and contact pages using the shared system without changing their content structure or behavior.
- Verify all five pages at desktop and mobile sizes, including menu, filters, form controls, loader, and browser console.
